## Migration mindset

Kubernetes is not only about container deployment. It changes how teams reason about release safety, health checks, rollback speed, and observability ownership.

## Patterns that improved our reliability

- Readiness and liveness probes aligned with real service behavior
- Autoscaling based on traffic profile, not generic defaults
- Safe rollout with progressive deployment and quick rollback plan

## Advice for team leads

Treat platform migration as a product. Define onboarding guides, runbooks, and shared conventions so every service team can operate consistently.
